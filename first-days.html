How to actually help in the first days

Most women in this country start breastfeeding. By the baby's first birthday, only about one in six still is. And the biggest thing driving women to stop isn't the milk — it's contradictory advice.

Your job in the first days isn't to fix anything. It's to know how it works — and guard her against the noise.

One page: six things to know, the single question that cuts through bad advice, and what you actually do. Written by Mark Harris, midwife of thirty years, and taken from Men, Love & Birth — the new edition.
