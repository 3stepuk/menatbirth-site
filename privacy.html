Privacy policy

Plain English, because there's nothing complicated going on here.

Who we are

This site is run by Mark Harris (Birthing Awareness Publishing), the author of Men, Love & Birth. Questions about your data: mark@birthing4blokes.com.

What we collect

The only personal data this site collects is the email address you give us when you ask for the free guide — and your name only if you choose to add it. We do not collect anything else. No tracking cookies, no analytics, no ads.

Why we collect it

To send you the free guide you asked for, and — only if you stay subscribed — occasional updates about the book and related work. That's the whole purpose.

How it's stored

Your email is held in Kit (formerly ConvertKit), our email service, under their secure systems and their data-processing terms. We never sell, rent, or share your details with anyone else.

Your rights

  • Unsubscribe — every email we send carries an unsubscribe link; one click and you're out.
  • Access and correction — ask us and we'll show you what we hold, and fix anything wrong.
  • Deletion — ask us (or just unsubscribe) and your details are removed.
  • These rights sit under UK GDPR. We're a small operation; the honest answer is usually "of course".

Changes

If this policy changes, this page changes — and if it matters, we'll tell you in an email.
