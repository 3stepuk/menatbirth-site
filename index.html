About the author

Thirty years in UK delivery rooms.

Mark Harris is a trained midwife with more than thirty years in birth work — a father of eight and grandfather of thirteen who has spent decades teaching men what actually helps in the birth room. Originally published by Pinter & Martin in 2015, this fully revised second edition is his own. He has a following of more than 12,000 across X, Instagram, LinkedIn and YouTube who turn to him for straight-talking, practical guidance on what actually helps in the birth room.
